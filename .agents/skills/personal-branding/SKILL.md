# Personal Branding for Engineers

## Strategic Framework
1. **The Hook**: Age/credential + definitive competence claim ("18yo AI Engineer @ IIT Jodhpur · Building production systems & contributing to SymPy").
2. **Proof Over Claims**: Every capability claim must cite a concrete repository, commit, or benchmark (e.g. 22 stars, atomic Lua sliding window, 25/25 tests).
3. **No Fluff**: Eliminate generic buzzwords ("passionate learner", "hard worker") in favor of technical verbs ("architects", "implements", "benchmarks").
