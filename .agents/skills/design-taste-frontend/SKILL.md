# Design Taste Frontend

## Taste Heuristics
- **Avoid**: Generic AI gradients, excessive saturated blur orbs, cookie-cutter purple cards, fake 3D blobs with no context.
- **Embrace**: Sharp high-contrast typography, monospace metric pills, deliberate grid alignment, subtle grain, and editorial asymmetrical layouts.
- **Materiality**: Realistic frosted glass (backdrop-blur-md with 1px border), clean inner shadows, precision corner radii (12px to 20px).
